What We Need From You

To provide accurate SAP assessments and EPCs, we'll need the following information depending on your project stage:

Design Stage SAP

  • Architectural plans and elevations
  • Building specifications
  • U-value calculations or construction details
  • Heating system specifications
  • Window and door schedules
  • Ventilation strategy

As-Built SAP & EPC

  • As-built drawings (if available)
  • Actual construction details
  • Installed heating system details
  • Actual window specifications
  • Any variations from design stage
  • Site access for inspection (if required)

General Information

  • Site address and postcode
  • Plot numbers (for developments)
  • Client contact details
  • Required completion date
  • Any specific compliance concerns
  • Building control reference (if applicable)

Don't worry if you don't have all of this information immediately - we can discuss your specific requirements and work with what's available. Desktop assessments can often proceed with the information you already have.
