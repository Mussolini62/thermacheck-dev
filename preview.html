What we need from you

Send as much of this as you can

Don't have it all yet? If you're at early design stage, we can advise on minimum specifications to achieve compliance.

Project information

  • Full property address with postcode
  • Site plan at 1:500 showing orientation

Drawings

  • Scaled floor plans of each storey (1:50)
  • Elevations for each elevation
  • Sections through the dwelling (1:50)

Construction

  • Walls, roof and floor build-ups, with insulation type and thickness
  • Window and door schedule, with U-values if known

Heating & hot water

  • Boiler or heat pump make and model
  • Cylinder size, emitters and controls

Ventilation

  • Strategy: natural, MEV or MVHR
  • Extractor fan details

Renewables

  • Solar PV or solar thermal details
  • Heat pump specification and other technologies
